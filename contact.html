❓ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions we receive before a first consultation.

Is the first consultation really free?
Yes, completely. Your initial case analysis — where our expert reviews your credit report, identifies all issues, and proposes a roadmap — costs nothing. You only pay if you choose to proceed with our service.
What documents do I need to provide?
To start, just your name and phone number. Once we connect, we'll ask for a copy of your credit report. If you don't have one, we'll guide you on how to download it free in minutes from the CIBIL or Equifax website.
How long does it take to repair a CIBIL score?
Personal detail errors (name, PAN, address) typically resolve in 15–30 days. Written-off accounts and legal entries take 45–60 days depending on the bank's response time. We give you a realistic timeline for your specific case during the free consultation.
Is credit repair legal in India?
Absolutely. Under RBI regulations, every Indian has the right to dispute inaccurate or unjust entries in their credit report. We use only these official, legally recognised dispute channels — nothing else. Our entire process is transparent and compliant.
What if my case can't be resolved?
Before we take your case, we tell you honestly whether we believe we can fix it. If we take it on and are unable to deliver the promised result, you receive a full refund under our money-back guarantee — no questions asked.
Can you help even if I have multiple issues on my report?
Yes. Many of our clients have 3–5 separate issues on their report. We handle everything in one engagement: written-off accounts, settled status, legal entries, and profile errors — across all four bureaus simultaneously.
