❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CIBIL scores, credit repair, and how we work.

What is a good CIBIL score for a home loan?
A score of 750 or above is considered excellent and secures the best home loan rates (7.5–9%). Scores of 700–749 attract slightly higher rates. Below 700, most banks either reject the application or charge 14–18% interest.
Is credit repair legal in India?
Absolutely. Our entire process is 100% legal and fully RBI-compliant. We file formal disputes with credit bureaus and lenders using established legal frameworks under the Credit Information Companies Regulation Act.
Can a written-off account really be removed?
Yes. A written-off account can be removed by settling the outstanding amount and obtaining a formal NOC, or by legally disputing the entry if it contains errors. Our legal team has successfully removed thousands with a 98% success rate.
What documents do I need to start?
You'll need your CIBIL report (we can help you get it), Aadhaar card, PAN card, and any relevant loan documents. During the free consultation we'll tell you exactly what's needed for your case.
How long does credit repair take?
Profile corrections take 15–20 days. Settled account conversions take 30–50 days. Written-off clearances take 45–60 days. Legal suit removals take 60–90 days. We provide weekly progress updates throughout.
What is the money-back guarantee?
If we cannot resolve the issue agreed upon in your service contract, we refund your full payment — no questions asked. This guarantee is spelled out explicitly in every service agreement.
Do you work with all four credit bureaus?
Yes. We file disputes simultaneously with all four — CIBIL (TransUnion), Equifax, Experian, and CRIF Highmark — so your credit profile is clean and consistent across every bureau that lenders check.
How much does your service cost?
Pricing starts at ₹3,999 for quick profile corrections, ₹9,999 for written-off or settled account clearance, and is custom-quoted for legal suit removal. All prices are one-time fees with no hidden charges.
