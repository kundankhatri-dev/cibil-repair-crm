02
Detailed Refund Conditions
✅2.1 — 100% Refund (No Work)

Cancel within 7 calendar days of payment and no work of any kind has commenced. "Work commenced" means: document review, credit analysis, consultation delivery, dispute letter preparation, or any email correspondence regarding your case. Minus payment gateway fees (2–5%).

⚠️2.2 — 75% Refund (Work Commenced)

If you cancel within 7 days but work has commenced as defined above, you receive 75% of the service fee. 25% is retained as an administrative fee covering document processing, credit analysis, consultation time, and resources already allocated to your case.

❌2.3 — 0% After 7 Days

No refund is issued if you cancel after the 7-day cooling-off period, regardless of work status. We do not offer pro-rata refunds for unused portions of service packages. Service may be transferred to another person with our prior written consent.

❌2.4 — 0% After Dispute Filing

No refund is issued after any dispute has been formally filed with any credit bureau or bank. Dispute filing represents significant work including document preparation, review, and submission — regardless of outcome.

⚠️2.5 — 50% for Our Filing Delay

Applies only if we fail to file any dispute within 60 calendar days of receiving complete documentation, the delay is solely our negligence, and you responded to all communications within 3 business days. Maximum 50% of service fee paid — total, not per dispute.

⚠️2.6 — 25% Partial Non-Resolution

Applies for a specific disputable item that was identified in our agreement, is demonstrably inaccurate, was disputed by us, and remains unresolved after 120 days solely through our fault. Maximum 25% of the proportionate fee for that item. Total cap: 25% of total fees paid.

03
Definition of a Valid Dispute

For refund purposes under Sections 2.5 and 2.6, a "Valid Dispute" is a credit report entry that meets all of the following criteria:

  • Factually inaccurate, incorrect, or belongs to another person entirely.
  • Legally expired — beyond 7 years for negative accounts (except bankruptcy: 10 years).
  • Not previously disputed by you or any other credit repair company.
  • You have provided documented proof of inaccuracy (e.g., a payment receipt showing the account was closed).

These are NOT valid disputes for refund purposes: accurate late payments, accurate defaults, accurate written-off accounts, accurate settlements, credit inquiries, credit utilization, or any negative information that is factually correct — regardless of how it affects your score.

04
No Refund Scenarios — Complete List

No refund shall be issued under any of the following circumstances:

  • Cancellation requested after the 7-day cooling-off period (except as provided in Sections 2.5 and 2.6).
  • Cancellation requested after any dispute has been filed with any credit bureau or bank.
  • Results are unfavorable due to accurate negative information on your credit report.
  • Credit bureaus or banks take longer than estimated to respond (30–60 days is typical).
  • You provided false, incomplete, or misleading information at any time during our engagement.
  • You fail to cooperate, respond to requests, or provide documents within 15 days of request.
  • You withdraw consent or cancel authorizations after services begin.
  • You directly contact credit bureaus or banks regarding disputes filed by us without our written consent.
  • Any service that has been partially or fully performed beyond the 7-day cooling-off window.
  • Change of mind, dissatisfaction with credit score outcome, or unmet expectations.
  • Loan rejection, credit denial, or interest rate increase by any lender based on your report.
  • Credit score decrease during our engagement — scores can and do fluctuate.
  • Force majeure events: credit bureau system failures, pandemics, natural disasters, government actions.

06
Refund Processing
  • Processing Timeline — Approved refunds are processed within 30 business days from the date of approval.
  • Refund Method — Refunds are credited to the original payment method used at the time of the transaction.
  • Gateway Fees Deducted — Payment gateway processing fees (typically 2–5%) are non-refundable and will be deducted from any refund amount, regardless of the refund percentage.
  • Bank Processing Time — An additional 3–7 business days may be required by your bank to reflect the refund in your account.
  • GST is Non-Refundable — All applicable taxes paid to the government cannot be refunded.

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank or payment provider without first requesting a refund through our designated email process:

  • All ongoing services will be suspended immediately and automatically.
  • You will forfeit eligibility for any refund under this policy.
  • We reserve the right to pursue collection of valid fees through legal means.
  • You agree to pay all collection costs, legal fees, and interest at 18% per annum if the chargeback is found unjustified.
  • We may file a dispute with credit bureaus regarding the chargeback transaction.

Initiating a chargeback without following our refund process is a breach of these Terms and may result in legal action.

Force Majeure

We shall not be liable for any delays or failure to perform our obligations caused by events beyond our reasonable control, including:

  • Credit bureau processing times (typically 30–45 days).
  • Bank or financial institution response times.
  • Postal, courier, or electronic transmission delays.
  • Government orders, natural disasters, pandemics, or epidemics.
  • Technical failures, internet outages, or cyber attacks.

No refunds are provided for delays caused by force majeure events. We will notify you promptly of any force majeure situation affecting your case.

This warranty applies only to items that reappear within 90 days and only when the reappearance is not caused by any action or inaction on your part.
