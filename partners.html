Clause 02
Commission Structure & Payment Terms

Partners earn commission on successful client referrals. Commission is payable ONLY after (a) client payment received in full, (b) service is completed, (c) client has not invoked the money-back guarantee. Payouts are processed on the 7th of every month via NEFT/IMPS. TDS deducted as per Indian tax laws.

  • Individual Partners: Up to 15% per conversion
  • DSA / Agency Partners: Up to 20% + volume bonuses
  • Corporate Partners: Custom structure, case-by-case
  • Platinum/Diamond Partners: Up to 50% on high volumes

Clause 03
Data Privacy, Security & IT Act Compliance

As a partner handling client personal and financial data, you are bound by the IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act 2023. You agree to:

  • Handle all client data with the highest standard of confidentiality.
  • Never share, sell, or transfer any client data to third parties without express written consent.
  • Immediately notify CIBIL Repair of any suspected data breach within 24 hours.
  • Delete all client data from personal devices upon termination of partnership.

Violation: Any breach may lead to criminal prosecution under Section 72A of the IT Act.

I agree to Data Privacy, Security & IT Act Compliance obligationsIncluding data handling standards, breach notification, and consequences.

Clause 04
Prohibited Activities & Compliance

The following activities are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate termination:

  • Deceptive Marketing: Misleading clients about outcomes, timelines, or guarantees beyond what is officially stated.
  • Impersonation: Representing yourself as an employee or officer of CIBIL Repair.
  • Brand Misuse: Modifying or using CIBIL Repair's logo/trademarks without written permission.
  • Competitor Promotion: Simultaneously promoting competing credit repair services.
  • Cash Collection: Collecting service fees from clients directly. All payments through official channels only.
I acknowledge and accept the Prohibited Activities & Compliance requirementsI understand violations may lead to immediate termination and legal action.

Clause 05
Termination, Suspension & Exit Policy

CIBIL Repair may terminate this partnership immediately, with or without cause, by written notice via email. Immediate termination may occur in case of fraud, data breach, or brand disrepute.

Upon termination, you must (a) immediately cease all referral activities, (b) remove all CIBIL Repair branding, (c) return or destroy all confidential information, (d) not approach CIBIL Repair clients directly for 12 months.

Commissions for conversions completed before termination will be paid per the regular schedule, subject to no fraud investigation.

I understand and accept the Termination & Exit PolicyIncluding post-termination obligations, notice periods, and pending commission treatment.

Governing Law: Laws of India. Disputes subject to exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Delhi, India. Disputes first attempted through mutual negotiation, then arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
