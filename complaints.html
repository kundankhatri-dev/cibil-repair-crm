Established Under Indian Consumer Law

This Grievance Redressal Mechanism is established in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. By filing a grievance, you agree to be bound by the terms outlined herein.

At CIBIL Repair ("Company", "we", "us", "our"), we are committed to resolving any complaints or grievances promptly, fairly, and in accordance with applicable laws. This page outlines the procedure to register a complaint and how we handle such matters.

01
Scope & Applicability

This Grievance Redressal Mechanism applies to:

👤Clients

All clients who have availed our credit repair services in any capacity.

🌐Website Users

All visitors and users of our website cibilrepair.in.

🤝Representatives

Any person who has interacted with our representatives in any channel.

🚫Exclusions

General service inquiries, pricing questions, and routine support that can be handled through regular customer care.

This mechanism does not apply to general service inquiries, pricing questions, or requests for information that can be handled through regular customer support channels.

02
Who Can File a Grievance?

Any client, partner, or visitor who has a genuine concern regarding our services, billing, data handling, or conduct may file a grievance. The following requirements are mandatory:

  • You must provide your full legal name and valid contact information.
  • A clear, detailed description of the issue is required for the grievance to be processed.
  • Your identity must be verifiable — anonymous complaints will not be entertained.
  • The incident must have occurred within the preceding 90 days of filing.

Anonymous or pseudonymous complaints cannot be processed. You must provide verifiable contact information for your grievance to be acknowledged and investigated.

04
How to File a Complaint?

You can submit your grievance through any of the following channels. The online form is recommended for fastest processing:

  • Online Form — Use the form above on this page. Fastest processing and automatic acknowledgment.
  • Email — Send a detailed email to grievance@cibilrepair.in with subject line "GRIEVANCE - [YOUR NAME]".
  • Registered Post — Send a written complaint to our registered address in Delhi NCR, India.

Required Information for a Valid Grievance: Full name and contact details · Clear description of the issue · Supporting documents (if any) · Desired resolution · Previous communication reference numbers.

05
Escalation & Resolution Timeline
📬Acknowledgment

Within 2 business days of receipt of complete information from you.

🔍Investigation

Thorough review of all facts, documents, and communications related to your complaint.

✅Resolution

Within 15 business days from acknowledgment. Complex matters may need up to 30 days.

📄Final Response

Provided in writing via email. Decision is final and binding unless challenged in court.

06
Grounds for Rejection of Grievance

We reserve the right to reject a grievance without further processing under the following circumstances:

  • Anonymous or pseudonymous complaints without verifiable identity.
  • Frivolous, vexatious, or malicious complaints intended to harass.
  • Complaints already resolved in previous correspondence.
  • Complaints outside the scope of our services.
  • Complaints containing discriminatory, abusive, or offensive language.
  • Complaints filed more than 90 days after the alleged incident.
  • Incomplete information that prevents meaningful investigation.
07
External Escalation Options

If you are not satisfied with the decision of our Grievance Officer, you may escalate to the following external authorities:

  • Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum (District Consumer Court) — under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
  • RBI Ombudsman — for banking or credit-related issues only.
  • Cyber Crime Cell — for issues related to cyber fraud or data breach.

Our Grievance Officer's decision does not prejudice your statutory legal rights. However, you must exhaust this internal mechanism before approaching external forums. Any legal proceedings shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Delhi NCR, India.

08
Confidentiality & Data Protection

All complaints and personal information shared during the grievance process will be kept strictly confidential. Your data will be:

  • Used exclusively for the purpose of investigating and resolving your grievance.
  • Processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Never shared with third parties except as required by law or to facilitate resolution.
  • Retained only for as long as required by applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
09
Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, CIBIL Repair's total liability arising from any grievance shall not exceed the total fees paid by you to us in the preceding 6 months. We shall not be liable for:

  • Any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
  • Delays in resolution caused by factors beyond our reasonable control.
  • Third-party actions, errors, or decisions by credit bureaus or lenders.
10
False or Malicious Complaints

Warning: Filing a false, frivolous, or malicious grievance with the intent to harass, defame, or cause harm to the Company or its representatives may result in serious consequences.

  • Immediate rejection of the grievance without further processing.
  • Suspension or termination of your service agreement.
  • Legal action for defamation or harassment under Indian law.
  • Reporting to relevant authorities including law enforcement.
  • Claim for damages, legal costs, and associated expenses.
11
Monthly Reporting & Transparency

We maintain an internal register of all complaints received. Summary data — number of complaints received and resolved — is available upon written request. Individual complaint details remain strictly confidential and are not disclosed publicly.

Our commitment to transparency means we track resolution rates and response times. To request summary grievance statistics, write to grievance@cibilrepair.in.

12
Amendments to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify, update, or change this Grievance Redressal Policy at any time without individual notice. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when changes were made. Your continued use of our services after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

13
Governing Law & Jurisdiction
  • Governing Law — This policy shall be governed by the laws of India.
  • Exclusive Jurisdiction — Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Delhi NCR, India.
  • Waiver of Forum — You expressly waive any objection to the venue of Delhi NCR courts.
