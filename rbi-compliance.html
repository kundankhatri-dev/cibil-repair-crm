05
Your Rights Under RBI Guidelines

As a consumer, you are protected by strong rights under Indian law and RBI regulations:

📄Free Credit Report

Right to access your credit report once per year for free from each Credit Information Company.

✏️Right to Dispute

Right to dispute inaccurate, outdated, or fraudulent information in your credit report at any time.

⏱️Timely Resolution

Right to know the status of your dispute within 30 days of filing with the bureau.

⚖️Escalation Rights

Right to escalate unresolved complaints to the Banking Ombudsman or Consumer Disputes Forum.

08
Regulatory Disclosures

Important Disclosures Required by Law: Please read the following carefully before engaging our services.

  • We are not affiliated with any bank, financial institution, or credit bureau (including CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, or CRIF).
  • Our services are provided on a "best effort" basis — no specific credit score improvement or negative item removal is guaranteed.
  • We do not charge any upfront fees for dispute filing without your explicit written consent.
  • All client funds are handled through secured payment gateways compliant with RBI norms.
  • We maintain an internal register of complaints as required by the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
  • We do not promise removal of accurate negative information — only inaccurate or erroneous items can be legitimately disputed.
  • Credit score improvement depends on multiple factors including your overall credit behavior and bureau responsiveness.
09
Banking Ombudsman Scheme

The Banking Ombudsman Scheme is a dispute resolution mechanism established by RBI for resolving complaints related to banking services. You may approach the Banking Ombudsman if:

  • Your complaint is not resolved within 30 days by our Grievance Officer.
  • Your complaint is rejected by us and you believe the rejection is unjustified.
  • You are not satisfied with the resolution provided by our Grievance Officer.

Banking Ombudsman Contact: Visit www.rbi.org.in or the Sachet Portal for the Banking Ombudsman office in your region.

10
Consumer Court Information

Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to file a complaint with the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum:

🏛️District Consumer Forum

For claims up to ₹50 lakhs. Most consumer complaints are resolved at this level.

🏢State Consumer Commission

For claims between ₹50 lakhs and ₹2 crores. Also handles appeals from District Forums.

🏅National Consumer Commission

For claims above ₹2 crores. Also handles appeals from State Commissions.

🌐Online Filing

File complaints online at edaakhil.nic.in — the National Consumer Disputes e-filing portal.
